## Platform-Specific Usage Notes

### Universal Compatibility (Most Platforms)
- **Slack, Discord, GitHub**: Support most `:shortcode:` formats listed above
- **Microsoft Teams**: Compatible with standard Unicode shortcodes
- **Reddit**: Uses similar shortcode system in markdown

### Platform Variations
- **Twitter/X**: Mix of native keyboard + some shortcode support
- **Instagram**: Primarily native emoji picker with limited shortcode recognition  
- **WhatsApp**: Native emoji keyboard, some platforms support shortcodes
- **Apple Devices**: Native keyboard + shortcode support in some apps
- **Android/Gboard**: Native suggestions + shortcode completion

### Categories Covered
- **Faces & Emotions** (Ranks 1-50): Most popular reaction emojis
- **Hearts & Symbols** (Throughout): Various colored hearts and symbolic elements
- **Nature & Plants** (Ranks 200+): Flowers, trees, and natural elements  
- **Food & Drink** (Ranks 250+): Popular food items and beverages
- **Animals** (Ranks 350+): Common animals and creatures
- **Activities & Objects** (Mixed): Sports, music, and lifestyle items

### Usage Statistics Source
Based on 2024 social media analytics showing 😭 (crying face) as the most-used emoji, surpassing 😂 (joy face). Rankings combine data from Twitter/X, Instagram, TikTok, WhatsApp usage patterns, and emoji keyboard frequency data.

### Shortcode Format
- Primary format: `:emoji_name:` (universal)
- Alternative names provided where commonly used
- Some platforms support multiple variations of the same shortcode
